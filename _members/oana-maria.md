## Short Bio

Oana is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computing at Imperial College London and a Principal Research Fellow in the Department of Computer Science at the University College London, where she previously held an Early Career Leverhulme Fellowship. She obtained her PhD in “Explaining Deep Neural Networks” from the Department of Computer Science at the University of Oxford. Her main research interests lie in AI safety, alignment, explainability, and natural language processing.
