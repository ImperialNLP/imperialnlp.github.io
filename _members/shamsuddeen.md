## Short Bio

I am a Google DeepMind Academic Fellow and Royal Society Career Development Fellow in the Department of Computing at Imperial College London. I work on multilingual NLP, computational social science, LLM alignment and AI safety. I build open datasets and benchmarks, and I evaluate how well large language models serve speakers of low-resource languages. I hold a Schmidt Sciences AI2050 Early Career Fellowship to make LLMs safe across cultures. My work has won nine paper awards, including at ACL, NAACL and AACL.
