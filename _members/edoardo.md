## Short Bio

Edoardo is an associate professor in natural language processing at Imperial College London. Before, he was an assistant professor at the University of Edinburgh and a visiting professor at NVIDIA. His research focuses primarily on efficient architectures for foundation models, especially with respect to adaptive memory, latent tokenization, and sparse attention. He was a visiting postdoctoral scholar at Stanford University and a postdoctoral fellow at Mila Montreal. In 2021, he obtained his PhD from the University of Cambridge. His research has been featured by the Economist and Scientific American, among others. He received a Google Research Faculty Award and several best paper awards and highlight awards at ACL, EMNLP, and Repl4NLP. He is recipient of an ERC Starting Grant and an ARIA Scaling Compute grant. He is a Scholar of the European Lab for Learning and Intelligent Systems (ELLIS) and part of the TACL journal editorial team.
